Let's hang at DrupalCon! Aaron Porcella Tue, 04/16/2024 - 13:43 no-flame April 16, 2024 Come for the tech, stay for the people! Hey there, fellow tech enthusiasts, aspiring Drupal wizards, and future collaborators! Are you ready to dive headfirst into the vibrant world of open source, community camaraderie, and unparalleled networking opportunities? Then mark your calendars, because DrupalCon Portland 2024 is just around the corner, and you won't want to miss out on the ultimate gathering of like-minded individuals ! From May 6th to May 9th, 2024, the Drupal community will descend upon the picturesque city of Portland for a celebration of all things open source. But let's be honest – while the sessions and workshops are undoubtedly fantastic, what truly sets DrupalCon apart is the people. Picture this: a bustling conference hall buzzing with excitement as developers, designers, marketers, and business owners from across the globe come together to share their passion for Drupal and collaborative innovation. Whether you're a seasoned veteran or a wide-eyed newcomer, there's something uniquely exhilarating about being surrounded by a community that shares your dedication to pushing the boundaries of what's possible with open-source technology. But DrupalCon isn't just about rubbing elbows with industry titans – it's also a golden opportunity to forge new connections, both personally and professionally. In an ever-evolving digital landscape, knowing the right people can make all the difference, whether you're seeking inspiration for your next project, scouting potential collaborators, or simply looking to expand your professional network. So why should you join us at DrupalCon Portland 2024? Here are just a few reasons: Collaboration: Dive headfirst into collaborative sessions, code sprints, and Birds of a Feather gatherings where you can brainstorm, problem-solve, and exchange ideas with fellow Drupal enthusiasts. Engagement: Immerse yourself in thought-provoking presentations, hands-on workshops, and interactive demos led by industry experts who are passionate about sharing their knowledge and expertise. Networking: Expand your professional circle by connecting with peers, mentors, and potential employers who share your values and vision for the future of open-source technology. Community: Experience the warmth and camaraderie of the Drupal community firsthand as you forge lifelong friendships and make memories that will last a lifetime. Whether you're a seasoned Drupal veteran or a curious newcomer eager to dip your toes into the world of open source, DrupalCon Portland 2024 promises to be an unforgettable experience filled with learning, laughter, and limitless possibilities. So pack your bags, polish your business cards, and get ready to embark on an adventure you'll never forget! Ready to join us? Visit the DrupalCon Portland 2024 website to register today, and let's make memories together at the ultimate gathering of Drupal devotees. See you in Portland! Yours in open-source solidarity, Rob Montero, Director of Engineering Zivtech.com
